## Implication / 对本项目的意义

The basic idea already exists. Our proposed focus is a small Chinese/English phone-first experience for students cooking at home: normalized Chinese ingredient aliases, cuisine selection, explained missing ingredients, and quick entry. This is a product direction, not a verified claim that existing projects lack these features.

同类产品已经存在。我们的重点放在中英食材同义词、家常菜、手机快速记录和能解释的推荐。这个定位是设计选择，不能据此声称其他产品都没有这些功能。简历价值来自自己实现、验证并解释功能和取舍。
